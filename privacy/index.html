Privacy Policy

How Lucernula handles your information.

What we collect

The current version of Lucernula does not collect personal data, and does not send what you read or do in the app to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

The PDFs you add, their covers, what Lucernula remembers about them — your reading position, your bookmarks, when you added and last read each one — and your display settings are stored in Lucernula's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. The PDFs are in a folder you can see in the Files app, under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) › Lucernula. If you back up your device to iCloud or to a computer, that backup includes Lucernula's storage, as it does every app's.

When Lucernula goes online

When you add a PDF from a link, Lucernula downloads that address directly from the website it points to. That website receives the request the way it would from a browser — including your device's IP address — and its own privacy policy applies. Lucernula keeps no cookies or cache from the download.

Lucernula Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account; Lucernula learns only whether Pro is yours and what it costs where you are, checked on your device. We receive no payment details and nothing that identifies you.

What we receive from Apple

If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can change that in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

If you email us, we use your address and message to answer you.

Changes

This policy describes the current version of Lucernula. If a later version handles your information differently, this policy and the privacy details on the App Store are updated before that version is released.

Contact

Lucernula is made by Peter Haldbæk in Denmark. Questions about privacy: support@briskbee.com.
