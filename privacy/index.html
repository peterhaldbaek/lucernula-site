Lucernula

Privacy Policy

Lucernula collects no data. Your PDFs and your reading stay on your device.

In short: no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Nothing you read or do in Lucernula is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The PDFs you add, their covers, and what Lucernula remembers about them — your reading position, your bookmarks, when you added and last read each one — and your display settings are stored only in Lucernula's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. The PDFs are in a folder you can see in the Files app, under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) › Lucernula.

If you back up your device to iCloud or to a computer, that backup includes Lucernula's storage, as it does every app's. That is between you and Apple; we cannot see it.

When Lucernula goes online

Only when you add a PDF from a link. Lucernula then downloads that address directly from the website it points to. That website receives the request the way it would from a browser — including your device's IP address — and its own privacy policy applies. Lucernula keeps no cookies or cache from the download, and tells us nothing about it.

Lucernula makes no other network connections.

What we receive

Nothing from the app. If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, which we use only to fix problems. You can change that in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you, and do not share them.

Children

Lucernula is suitable for everyone and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. A change that meant collecting any data would be stated plainly in the app's release notes as well.

Contact
