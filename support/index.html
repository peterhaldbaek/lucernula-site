Support

Help with Lucernula, the PDF reader for iPhone and iPad.

Something not working, or an idea?

Email support@briskbee.com. Say which device and iOS version you use, and what you expected to happen.

Adding PDFs

  • From the Files app: tap + in the Library, then Choose from Files.
  • From another app: open the PDF in Mail, Safari or any app that shows it, tap Share, and choose Lucernula.
  • From a link: tap +, then Add from Link, and enter the address of a PDF on the web.
  • On iPad: drag PDFs from another app straight into the Library.

Where are my PDFs?

In the Files app, under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) › Lucernula. A PDF you put in that folder appears in the Library the next time you open the app, and one you remove from it leaves the Library.

Reading

  • Swipe sideways to turn the page. Tap anywhere on the screen to show or hide the controls.
  • Your place is saved on every page turn, and Continue reading in the Library takes you back to it.
  • Pinch to zoom. Every page of that PDF then opens zoomed the same way; pinch back out for the whole page. Each PDF keeps its own zoom, on each device.
  • The half-filled circle at the bottom right sets the paper: Light, Grey or Dark. Dark turns the whole page dark for reading at night.
  • The contents button shows the book's chapters, your bookmarks and every page as a thumbnail. Chapters appear only when the PDF itself has a table of contents.

Common questions

A PDF says it is locked. It is password-protected, and Lucernula cannot open password-protected PDFs yet.

How do I remove a PDF? Touch and hold it in the Library and choose Delete. Its reading position and bookmarks are deleted with it.

A link won't add. The address has to lead straight to a PDF file. A web page that shows a PDF inside it, or one that needs you to sign in first, won't work — download the PDF in Safari and share it to Lucernula instead.

Does Lucernula sync between my devices? With Lucernula Pro, yes: your PDFs, your place in each and your bookmarks sync through your own iCloud to every iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account, and stay on each one to read offline. Turn it on or off in Settings (the gear in the Library) › Sync with iCloud.

How do I get Lucernula Pro, or restore it? Tap + in the Library, then Lucernula Pro. It is a one-time purchase; Restore Purchases is on the same screen. The free library keeps 5 PDFs, and nothing already in it is ever locked.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later. Lucernula also runs on Mac computers with Apple silicon and on Apple Vision Pro, as an iPad app.
